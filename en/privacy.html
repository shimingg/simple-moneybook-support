Privacy policy

You manage your ledger. This policy explains the data Simple Moneybook handles, where it is stored, and how you can manage or delete it.

1. About the app

Simple Moneybook is an app for manually recording income, expenses, accounts, and categories. You do not need to create an app account for everyday use.

The app does not connect to banks, cards, or financial institutions. It does not automatically obtain your actual financial transactions or make payments on your behalf.

2. Data the app handles

Account and category names, amounts, currencies, dates, notes, and records that you enter are used to save your ledger, calculate recorded balances, and show statistics. Language, appearance, preferred currency, and default account settings are stored on the device.

When you enable iCloud sync, the app also stores sync status, a sync baseline, and an iCloud service account identifier locally to recognize the same account and avoid mixing ledgers from different accounts. The app does not ask for your Apple Account password.

3. Local storage and service providers

Your ledger is primarily stored on your device. Custom names and notes retain your original text and are not sent to an online translation service. The current app has no ads, third-party analytics SDK, developer-operated ledger server, or cross-app tracking.

The app uses Apple system services for iCloud, file selection, sharing, and related features. Apple's services, device protections, and system backups operate under their settings and privacy policies. Local storage does not mean data can never be included in system backups you enable.

4. Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. When you enable it, accounts, categories, transactions, and identifiers needed for ledger history sync to your Apple Account's CloudKit private database. Device language, appearance, preferred currency, and default account settings are not part of ledger sync.

Sync updates both the local and cloud ledger; it is not a history of backup versions. Turning sync off keeps existing local and cloud data. Changing your Apple Account stops the original ledger's sync. Account migration is not currently available.

5. Export, restore, and recovery backups

You can export accounts, categories, and transactions as a JSON file and choose a destination in Files. Exported backups have no additional app password encryption, so store them carefully. Files saved to a third-party storage service are also subject to that service's access controls and policies.

Before restoring, the app validates the file, shows a preview, asks for confirmation, and saves a recovery backup of the current ledger. Sync is turned off after restoration. If you enable it again and cloud data differs, you must resolve the difference. Damaged source data may be retained as a separate copy that can be exported or deleted but cannot be directly restored.

Recovery backups stay on the device and are not automatically removed. You can delete individual or all recovery backups in Settings → Sync & backup. Deletion cannot be undone and does not affect the current ledger or files you exported elsewhere.

6. Retention and deletion

The ledger remains on the device until you edit or delete records, restore another ledger, or remove the app's data through the system. Deleting an account or category retains associations or deletion markers needed for ledger history; it does not erase all historical information. Deleting an item does not also delete earlier backups, copies on other devices, or system backups.

To clear the cloud ledger, first turn sync off on your other devices, then confirm Clear iCloud ledger in Settings → Sync & backup. This device turns sync off before deleting this app's cloud ledger in the current account. The local ledger, recovery backups, and exported files remain. If completion cannot be confirmed, you can retry; sync cannot be enabled while deletion is pending.

After successful deletion, enabling sync again requires confirmation to upload the local ledger. A later upload from another device can also recreate cloud data. This action does not delete your Apple Account, other apps' data, or Apple system backups.

To remove local app data, use Delete App in iOS, rather than Offload App. First manage any cloud ledger and backups you want to delete. Deleting the app alone does not clear an existing iCloud ledger, exported files, or data on other devices. Manage exported files and system backups separately where they are stored.

7. Support and website data

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your sender address, message, and any content you choose to attach. This information is used to respond, handle privacy requests, and improve the app. Include only information needed to resolve the issue; do not send passwords or your full ledger.

Support correspondence is retained while handling the issue and necessary follow-up. You may use the same support address to request access or deletion. If applicable law or dispute handling requires some information to be retained, the reason will be explained.

These information pages contain no advertising or analytics tracking scripts. Website and email service providers may process IP addresses, browser information, or email technical data to operate and secure their services. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

8. Policy updates

Material changes to this policy will be announced on this page. For privacy questions, contact support.
